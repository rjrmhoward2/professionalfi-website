Pre-Launch Information for Healthcare Providers

Platform Launch Timeline +
ProfessionalFi is currently in development and scheduled to launch between Q4 2026 and Q1 2027. Early access applications are being accepted now. All pricing, features, and timelines are subject to change before the official launch.
MyBodyGuard Guardian Requirement +
ProfessionalFi requires patients to use MyBodyGuard Guardian for their health data. We do not store patient health data on our servers—patients own it via their Guardian. Providers access patient data with explicit permission through secure, HIPAA-compliant channels.
Protocol Marketplace & Revenue +
Providers keep 70% of protocol sales revenue, with 15% going to ProfessionalFi and 15% to MyBodyGuard. The protocol marketplace will launch concurrently with the platform (Q4 2026). Revenue projections shown in the calculator are estimates based on industry benchmarks and are not guaranteed. Actual earnings will vary based on protocol quality, pricing, marketing, and market demand.
Compliance Certifications +
ProfessionalFi is being built with compliance-first architecture. HIPAA compliance certification is targeted for Q2 2026, GDPR compliance is in progress, SOC 2 Type II is planned for Q4 2026, and HITRUST certification (Enterprise plans only) is planned but not yet scheduled. All certifications will be completed before the platform handles any Protected Health Information (PHI).
Data Sovereignty & Patient Ownership +
ProfessionalFi is part of the MyBodyGuard.ai ecosystem, which prioritizes data sovereignty. Patient health data is stored in MyBodyGuard Guardians (personal health data vaults), not on ProfessionalFi servers. Patients maintain complete ownership and control of their health data. Providers access data only with patient permission. We do not sell, mine, or monetize patient data.
Pricing & Plan Changes +
All pricing displayed on this page is preliminary and subject to change before the official launch. Early access applicants will be notified of final pricing before being asked to commit to a plan. Volume discounts, custom enterprise pricing, and promotional offers may be available at launch.
